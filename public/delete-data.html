Delete my data

Your attendance data belongs to your company. To ask for your data to be deleted or corrected:

  1. Ask your company's administrator, who can act on it in the app; or
  2. write to support@samarthops.in with your name, your company and the e-mail address you sign in with.

The company decides, because it may have a duty to keep some records. Backups age out after 56 days. See the privacy notice, section "Your rights and choices".
